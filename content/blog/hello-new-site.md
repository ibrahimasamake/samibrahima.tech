Welcome to the new samibrahima.tech!

The old site was a CV with a few stories. This one is built for the people we actually want to meet: shop owners, founders and teams who have an idea, a problem, or a product that should work better.

## What's new

- **[Services](/services/)**: websites, mobile apps, custom business software, online shops, cloud and AI, explained in plain words.
- **[Case studies](/projects/)**: Sougba, Fere, Nafas Perfumes, RasteScan and more. The real problem, what was built, how, and what changed.
- **[Start a project](/start-a-project/)**: five quick steps instead of an awkward "hi, how much for an app?" email.
- **This blog**: architecture notes, tools we like, things that broke, and news from what we are building.

## What to expect here

Short, practical posts. No buzzword soup. Stories from real products: the pipeline that deploys Sougba, the morning a clean-up job deleted the wrong data, why the AI in RasteScan is never allowed to decide alone.
